Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what information this website collects, why, and what choices you have. It applies to scriptlinestudio.com.

1. Contact form

When you submit the contact form, the information you enter (name, company if provided, email, phone, and your message) is sent directly to our email inbox via a third-party form service, Formspree. We use this only to reply to you about your inquiry. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't share this information with anyone else.

2. Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which parts of the site they use. This only runs if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you click "Decline," or don't interact with the banner, no analytics cookies are set and no visit data is collected.

CookiePurposeDuration
_ga, _ga_*Google Analytics: distinguishes visitors, measures site usageUp to 2 years

You can change your cookie choice at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of the site.

3. What Google Analytics sees

If you accept cookies, Google Analytics receives general information like which pages you visited, roughly how long you stayed, what device/browser you're using, and an approximate location (usually city-level, based on IP address; we don't see your exact address). It does not receive your name or contact details unless you separately submit them through the contact form.

4. Third-party services this site uses

  • Formspree, which processes contact form submissions. See their own privacy policy at formspree.io.
  • Google Analytics, for visit tracking, only after consent. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website. GitHub may log basic server access data (like IP addresses) as part of normal web hosting; see GitHub's own privacy statement.

5. Your choices

  • You can decline analytics cookies entirely and still use the whole site normally.
  • You can change your cookie choice anytime via the footer link.
  • You can ask us to delete any message you've sent through the contact form by emailing us directly.

6. Changes to this policy

If what this site collects or how it's used changes, this page will be updated with a new date at the top.

Questions?

Message us directly through the contact form or email if you have any questions about your data.
